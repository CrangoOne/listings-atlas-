Tile Lab

Camera

Wheel zooms, drag pans. Tap Multi to select many objects; drag a box, then drag the selection to move (any layers).

Axes

Back corner is the top. +x runs toward the front-right, +y toward the front-left.

Layers

The picture stays the whole room. The grid and the selected cell show the Show-layer filter. On Rugs, a tap on the rug picture selects that rug. The bar is an Object — removing it leaves the floor. On top is for a glass on the bar. Stack order: Floor, Rugs, Objects, On top.

Pieces

Folders follow stack layers. Wood / Stone / Proposal A keep matching floor and wall tiles together. Objects split into Bar, Counter, and Furniture.

Edits

Edits stick on this phone. The first change also pins a private base so a later catalog publish does not wipe your layout. Keep this scene folds the picture into that base; Reset to catalog drops the pin. Copy edits for chat (put/erase/lock/unlock); Copy level JSON when you want the agent to land the scene in git.

Cells map

Same diamond as the picture: +x front-right, +y front-left. Blue is the wall ring, brown is floor.
